who you are

You make hard days look easy

You carry deadlines, people, and now me — all in the same breath, without ever making it look like it costs you anything. You're patient with everyone except yourself. You listen before you speak, and forgive before you're even asked. I don't say this enough, but I see all of it. Every quiet, unglamorous piece of it.

why you matter

You're the calm in most of my days

Somewhere between the ordinary days and the messy ones, you became the person I check in with before anyone else — not because I have to, but because everything feels steadier the moment you know about it. You're not an add-on to my life, Zainny. You're one of the actual reasons it makes sense.

since you left for college

The quiet has been loud

I miss the randomness most — the meetups we never planned, the drives with no real destination, just you, me, and whatever song was playing too loud. My phone feels different now. Not empty, just quieter. I'm proud of you for being where you need to be. I just haven't figured out how to miss you and be happy for you at the same time. Still working on that one.

how you love me

You never fight fire with fire

Even today — on a day that was supposed to be about someone taking care of you — you still found room to worry about me. That's just who you are. Calm when I'm not. Soft when I'm sharp. You pamper me on days I haven't earned it, and today I noticed it more than ever. I always notice, even when I don't act like it.

what I want to do

Let me spoil you back, for once

I want to be the reason your day gets lighter, not heavier. I want to show up with the things you never ask for — your favorite food after a long lecture, a call at exactly the right time, the same patience you keep handing me, given right back. I love you in the loud, obvious ways, and in the quiet ones nobody sees. I want you to feel both.

about today

I'm sorry. No excuses.

I shouted at you today over nothing real, on a day you were already dealing with pain I can't fully understand. That's on me, not you. You were never the problem — my inability to sit with my own frustration was. You don't have to manage my emotions for me. I do. I'm sorry I made a hard day harder for you. You deserved better from me today, and I know it.

what changes now

Small promises, kept quietly

  • I'll pause before I react, not after.
  • I won't raise my voice when I'm actually just overwhelmed.
  • I'll check in on your hard days, not just the easy ones.
  • I'll own my mood instead of handing it to you.
  • I'll say sorry faster than I did today.
just so you know

Nothing about us changed today

One bad moment doesn't undo everything good between us. You're still my person. This is still safe. I'm not going anywhere, and neither is how much I love you. Today was a mistake, not a pattern — and I plan to prove that, not just say it.
